Why you should be there

What you will gain

Three days set apart for you. Come expecting, and leave with more than you came with.

  • Fresh fire and renewalBe refreshed by the Holy Spirit and leave stirred up for the season ahead.
  • Clarity and directionHear what God is saying for your family, work and purpose in the new things He is doing.
  • Teaching that equipsReceive the Word from Rev. M.G Kafaru and Pst. Moses Oyerinde and carry it home.
  • Worship in His presenceJoin thousands in praise led by Kay Wonders and experience the presence of God together.
  • Prayer and ministrationStand in faith with ministers and fellow believers for breakthrough in your life and home.
  • One family, many branchesMeet believers from Lagos, Ibadan, Delta, Abeokuta and beyond, and build lasting fellowship.

The word for this season

Two promises anchor the 2026 convention. Come expecting.

Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.ZECHARIAH 4:6 · KJV
Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.ISAIAH 43:19 · KJV
