Who we are

We are a family of believers in Lagos, gathered around one message: Jesus Christ is Lord, and his good news is for everyone, everywhere.

EuangelionGreek · εὐαγγέλιον

The word behind "Evangel". It means good news, the message of what God has done through Christ.

The TorchOur seal · Matthew 5:14

An open Bible held high with a living flame over the globe. The Word is our light, and we carry it to the world.

International MissionMatthew 28:19

Rooted in Lagos, sent to the nations. Every member is a messenger.

Meet our General Overseer

Pastor Moses Oyerinde

General Overseer · Affectionately known as P.M.O.

“Raising Christ-like achievers globally.”

Pastor Moses Oyerinde is an influential Nigerian Christian minister, prophetic intercessor and spiritual leader. His mandate is to raise Christ-like achievers across the world, and he is a prominent voice in contemporary Nigerian campus evangelism and prophetic prayer movements.

First time?

You're welcome here.

A few answers for your first visit. Ushers at the door will help you find a seat.

What happens in a service?

Praise and worship, prayer, the reading and preaching of the Word, and a time for testimonies and thanksgiving.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some members dress up for Sunday; you'll be welcome either way.

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Children's Church runs alongside the main service with teachers who love kids.

How do I become a member?

Fill the welcome card after service or speak with a pastor. We'll invite you to a short foundation class.
